What it does

Shadowing means hearing a sentence in the language you are learning and saying it straight back, again and again. The tedious part is the editing. This does that part.

  1. Paste a link or drop in a fileA video URL, or audio you already have — mp3, wav, m4a, flac, ogg, aac. Queue as many as you like.
  2. It transcribes the speechA Whisper engine finds every sentence and exactly where it starts and ends. It can write an English translation alongside, and strip backing music out of the way first.
  3. It cuts and repeatsEach sentence becomes its own clip, played as many times as you choose, with a gap after each one scaled to the sentence — long enough to say it back.
  4. You get one video to practise withEvery sentence in order, optionally with the text on screen. Play it anywhere: phone, TV, in the car.

Yours to adjust

Repeats and gaps

How many times each sentence plays, and how long the silence after it lasts relative to the sentence.

Sentence length

Long sentences are split on a natural pause, so nothing outruns your working memory.

Text on screen

Burn in the transcript, with an English translation underneath if you want one.

Accuracy against speed

Choose the Whisper model size. Bigger models catch more, and take longer.

Several at once

Queue a batch and let it work through them; two run side by side by default.

Try before committing

Cap a job to the first few minutes to check your settings on a long video.

Installing it

  1. Run the installerIt installs just for you, inside your own user folder — no administrator password needed.
  2. Click past the Windows warningThe installer is not code-signed, so SmartScreen shows a blue “Windows protected your PC” box. Choose More info, then Run anyway.
  3. Let it fetch the transcription engineThe first time you open the app it offers to download the Whisper engine — about 1.4 GB, unpacking to roughly 4.5 GB. That happens once, inside the app, with a progress bar.

An NVIDIA graphics card makes this much faster. Transcribing and rendering both use the GPU when there is one. It works without, it just takes considerably longer.

On a Mac, open it the first time with right-click › Open — before you try it any other way. Drag the app out of the disk image into Applications, then right-click it and choose Open. The app is signed, but not notarised by Apple, so Gatekeeper will not vouch for it: double-clicking a freshly downloaded copy does not give you the permission prompt, and macOS may run it from a temporary read-only copy rather than the one you installed. Going through Open once gives you a button to allow it, and from then on it launches normally from Applications. The Mac build is for Apple Silicon, and transcribes on the CPU.

The details

Runs on
Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit — or macOS on Apple Silicon
Installs to
%LOCALAPPDATA%\Programs\Henrys Shadowing App — per user, no admin rights. On a Mac, wherever you drag the app; /Applications is the usual place.
Comes with
FFmpeg, yt-dlp and Deno, bundled so there is nothing else to install. The exact pinned versions are listed in each release’s notes.
Downloads later
The Faster-Whisper transcription engine, on first run, into %LOCALAPPDATA%\henrys_shadowing_app
Disk space
About 400 MB installed, plus roughly 5 GB once the engine is unpacked
Uninstalling
Settings › Apps › Installed apps, like anything else. It asks whether to delete the downloaded engine too, and keeps it unless you say otherwise.
Checksum
Every release ships a .sha256 file next to the installer.
